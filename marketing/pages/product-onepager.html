Why Motiva

Beat the morning scroll. Build momentum that lasts.

You wake up, grab your phone, and an hour disappears. By the time you sit up, the guilt is already louder than the to-do list. Sound familiar? Motiva is built for that exact moment.

Instead of another planner that just shows you what you're not doing, Motiva turns every task into a small win — with progress you can see and personal rewards you actually look forward to. It's not about doing more. It's about finally starting.

What makes Motiva different

Other apps stop at points.
Motiva lets you choose what those points unlock.

Points, XP and badges are fine — until you realise they don't actually buy you anything. Motiva still tracks your progress with points, but the difference is what's on the other side: a real reward you defined yourself, tied to the task you're about to start.

Typical productivity apps

Points are the whole reward

What you earn: points, XP or badges that live inside the app and don't unlock anything real.

Motivation: an external scoreboard — climb the leaderboard, keep the number going up.

Personalization: the same badges for everyone, whether you care about them or not.

The payoff: a higher score… and then what?

Motiva

Points plus a reward you actually want

What you earn: points and a real reward you set yourself — coffee, gaming time, an episode, a break.

Motivation: internal — you're working toward something you chose, not just a number.

Personalization: every task tied to a reward that fits your real life.

The payoff: finish the task, cash in the thing you were actually looking forward to.

Features

Tasks, broken down

Big assignments become small subtasks. Every check-off counts, so starting never feels like climbing a wall.

Progress that feels good

Make it yours

Pick an avatar, set up your profile, and track your wins over time. Motiva grows with you, not against you.

How it works

From "I'll do it later" to done — in three steps.

1

Add a task & pick the reward

Pop in what you need to do today, set a deadline, and choose a reward you actually want — not points, not a badge.

2

Break it down

Split it into subtasks small enough that starting feels easy. Check them off as you go.

3

Cash in the reward you set

Watch the progress bar fill up, celebrate the win, and enjoy the real reward you chose at the start.
